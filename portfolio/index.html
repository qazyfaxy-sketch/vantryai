The five-second problem

Three ways a site quietly loses the sale.

Visitors decide almost instantly whether to stay. Most small business sites fail on the same three things — and every one of them is fixable.

01Speed

Slow load

Heavy images, bloated themes and plugins nobody uses. People leave before the page even appears.

✓Fast by default. Lean builds, optimized images, nothing that doesn’t earn its place.
02Clarity

Unclear offer

“Welcome to our website” tells a visitor nothing. They need to know what you do and who it’s for — immediately.

✓Say it plainly. A headline that names the service, the place, and why you.
03Action

No next step

Buried contact forms, five competing buttons, a phone number in the footer. Interested people get stuck.

✓One obvious move. Book, call, buy — always in reach, on every screen size.

FAQ

Good questions.

Don’t see yours? Send a message — I reply with a straight answer.

Which platform should I use? +

Framer is great for polished, fast landing pages. Webflow suits content-heavy or design-led sites. WordPress makes sense if you already have one or need its plugin ecosystem. I’ll recommend whichever you’ll find easiest to maintain.

What do I need to send you? +

Your content (text, logo, photos — rough is fine) and a few sites you like. If your copy isn’t ready, I can help shape it so it’s clear and gets to the point.

Can you redesign my existing site? +

Yes. Redesigns of sites that look dated or don’t work on mobile are a big part of what I do. I’ll keep what works, fix what doesn’t, and make sure nothing important breaks in the move.

Will I be able to update it myself? +

That’s the goal. Every project ends with a short walkthrough showing you how to change text, images and pages — no developer needed for everyday edits.

What if we’re not a good fit? +

Then I’ll say so before you spend anything. Send me a message with what you’re building and I’ll tell you honestly whether I’m the right person for it.
